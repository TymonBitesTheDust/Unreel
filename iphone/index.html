Unreel

No Shorts, no Reels, on iPhone

iPhone doesn't let apps look inside other apps, so Unreel works on youtube.com and instagram.com in Safari. The Shorts and Reels feeds are gone; search, long videos, posts, Stories and messages keep working.

Set it up (2 minutes)

1
Get the free Userscripts app It's open source, needs iOS 15 or later, and runs small scripts in Safari. Open it once after installing.
Open in the App Store
2
Turn it on in Safari Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts, turn on Allow Extension, and set All Websites to Allow.

On iOS 17 or earlier: Settings → Safari → Extensions.

3
Install Unreel's script Open the link below in Safari. A page of code appears; that's normal. Tap the AA (or puzzle-piece) button in the address bar, choose Userscripts, then Install.
Open unreel.user.js

Userscripts checks for new versions by itself. If you installed Unreel before version 1.1, install it once more to get updates.

4
Use the websites Type youtube.com or instagram.com into Safari's address bar. The Shorts tab, Shorts shelves and the Reels tab are gone. A Short someone sends you opens as a normal video, and a Reel as a normal post.

Make it stick

Delete the YouTube and Instagram apps

The simplest way. While an app is installed, iPhone opens youtube.com and instagram.com links in the app, where nothing can remove the feeds. Without it, every link opens in Safari.

Add them to your Home Screen, with a pause

Like the pause card on Android: each time you open YouTube or Instagram from these icons, Unreel counts it and waits a few seconds before letting you in. Turn back and it counts that too.

YouTube with a pause Instagram with a pause
  1. Open one of the buttons above in Safari.
  2. Tap ⋯ next to the address bar, then Share. (On older iPhones, the Share button is at the bottom.)
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, turn Open as Web App off so it opens in Safari where Unreel works, and tap Add.
Keeping the apps? Pause them with Shortcuts

An automation sends you to the pause page whenever the YouTube or Instagram app opens. From there you can go to the website, where the feeds are gone.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap Automation, then + (or New Automation).
  2. Choose App, select YouTube, keep Is Opened, and pick Run Immediately. Tap Next.
  3. Tap New Blank Automation, add the action Open URLs, and paste this link into it:
pause/?app=youtube&from=app
  1. Make a second automation for Instagram with this link:
pause/?app=instagram&from=app
If the app opens again when you continue to the website: iPhone sends those links to the installed app. The pause page notices and tells you; deleting the app is the fix. On iOS 16 or earlier, turn off Ask Before Running instead of choosing Run Immediately.
Or lock the apps with Screen Time

Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit, choose YouTube and Instagram, set 1 minute, and turn on Block at End of Limit. Ask a friend to set the Screen Time passcode.

Privacy

The script runs only on youtube.com and instagram.com, inside Safari, and sends nothing anywhere. The pause pages keep their counts in Safari on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.
